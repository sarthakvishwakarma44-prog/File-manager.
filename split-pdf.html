Frequently asked questions

How do I type page ranges?

Use commas and dashes, for example 1-3, 5, 8-10 gives pages 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 9 and 10.

What do I get when I split every page?

A ZIP file with one PDF per page, named in order.

Can I split a password-protected PDF?

No. Remove the password first, then split the file.
